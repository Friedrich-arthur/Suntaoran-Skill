# Interview Playbook / 采访脚本

Use this playbook before generating any hiring kit.

生成任何招聘作战包前，先按这份脚本采访。

## Required Inputs / 必问核心项

Do not generate until all three are known:

拿到以下三项之前不要生成：

1. Role and scope / 岗位与范围
   Ask: What is the role name? What responsibilities are in scope? Who does this person report to? Will they manage people?

2. Business problem / 解决什么问题
   Ask: Why hire now? What burden, risk, growth bottleneck, or operational gap should this person solve?

3. Talent profile / 人才类型
   Ask: Senior expert or high-potential builder? What 3-5 traits are mandatory? What hard requirements exist for experience, background, tools, industry knowledge, or AI fluency?

## Defaultable Inputs / 可默认项

If company config provides defaults, use them and label assumptions:

如果公司配置提供默认值，可以先用并标注为假设：

- salary and incentives / 薪资与激励；
- location and work mode / 地点和办公方式；
- travel expectations / 出差要求；
- JD tone / JD 语气；
- output format / 产出形态；
- publishing channels / 投放渠道。

## Confirmation Prompt / 确认提示

Before generating, summarize:

生成前先汇总：

```markdown

- Scope / 范围:
- Business problem / 要解决的问题:

- Assumptions from company config / 来自公司配置的假设:

Confirming this, I will generate the full hiring kit.
请确认。确认后我会生成完整招聘作战包。
```
