# Company Config Example / 公司配置示例

Copy this file to `company-config.md` and fill in private company defaults. Do not commit `company-config.md`.

复制为 `company-config.md` 后填写私有公司默认值。不要提交 `company-config.md`。

```yaml
company_name: "Your Company"
one_liner: "One sentence explaining what the company does"
mission: "Optional mission statement"
values:
  - "Value 1"
  - "Value 2"
  - "Value 3"

who_we_want: |
  Describe the traits you want across hires.
  Example: self-driven, reliable, AI-literate, fact-oriented, low-ego.

who_we_dont_want: |
  Describe traits that are poor fits.
  Example: passive, deadline-insensitive, politics-first, unwilling to learn.

north_star: "Optional operating principle, e.g. customer success defines success"
salary_default: "Default compensation wording or range logic"
location: "City, region, hybrid, remote, or onsite"
market_context: "Target market and industry context"
tone: "Professional, warm, direct, founder-led, etc."
channels:
  - "Referrals"
  - "Job board"
  - "LinkedIn"

legal_or_compliance_notes: |
  Any employment-law, data-privacy, industry, or compliance constraints.
```
